# AGENTS.md — Vibe Marketing First

## What This Repo Is

This is the **Vibe Marketing First** playbook — the alternative startup approach for 2026. The core thesis: **don't learn to code, learn to sell first.**

This repo is the companion to the article: [How to Build an App from Scratch — The Marketing-First Approach](https://axelfreeman.com/blog/vibe-coding-guide.html)

## The Philosophy

The mainstream advice — "learn to code, build an MVP, launch fast" — is backwards. In 2026, everyone can vibe code. A working app is not a competitive advantage; it's table stakes. The scarce resource is **distribution**: the ability to exchange words, images, and video for human attention.

Three classic mistakes every beginner makes:

| # | Mistake |
|---|---------|
| 1 | Picking a market with no buyers — cool idea, nobody pays |
| 2 | Building 60% of what the market needs — missing the other 40% from inexperience |
| 3 | Expecting users in month 1 — marketing takes 6 months of grinding |

The fix: **spend the first 3 months on marketing instead of coding.** Learn to attract attention first. Code later. Once you have distribution, building the product turns it into a real business.

## The 4 AI Prompts

This repo contains four deep-research AI prompts designed to be copy-pasted into ChatGPT, Claude, or DeepSeek. Each prompt is standalone and can be run independently, though they work best as a pipeline:

| # | Prompt | Purpose |
|---|--------|---------|
| 1 | **Validate Your Idea (The Brutal Truth)** | Market validation: search for real spending data, do the math, identify scams, list 5 reasons your idea will fail, assess solo founder viability, deliver a final verdict |
| 2 | **Find Your Audience and Where They Buy** | Market intelligence: identify who's already paying, build audience portraits, find their communities, map competitor traffic sources, create a realistic communication plan with budgets |
| 3 | **Write Your Sales Proposal** | Copywriting: generate 100/300/800 character sales pitches for every platform — Telegram DMs, cold emails, landing pages, pitch decks |
| 4 | **Find Your First Users — From 1 to 10** | User acquisition: brand hijacking, community sniping, direct poaching — steal 9 users from competitors with a 3-week timeline and specific, executable actions |

These prompts encode the "marketing first" pipeline: validate → find audience → craft the pitch → acquire users. Only after completing these steps should someone consider writing code.
